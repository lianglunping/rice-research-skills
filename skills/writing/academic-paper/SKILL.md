# Academic Paper Writing

Choose the requested mode: plan, outline, full draft, revision, revision coaching,
abstract, literature review, citation check, disclosure or format conversion.
Single-section work does not require a full pipeline.

1. Identify paper type, audience or venue, scientific question, supplied evidence,
   language, output format and actual scope. Clarify only blocking gaps.
2. Map claims to sources, biological units, cohorts and evidence levels. Separate
   observations, inference and hypotheses. Do not invent data or citations.
3. Draft or revise the requested sections with a coherent argument. Use a compact
   outline when it resolves a structural uncertainty; preserve approved structure.
4. Verify source identity and support, methods/results consistency, figure/table
   references, limitations and disclosure as applicable. Report unresolved items.
5. Deliver the requested text or file and inspect the exported artifact when
   formatting is part of the task. Submission requires explicit authorization.

Read only the references needed for the chosen task:
- [Paper structure](references/paper_structure_patterns.md)

- [Abstract writing](references/abstract_writing_guide.md)

Use supplied writing examples as a soft style guide; discipline and evidence
take priority. Installed prompts and templates are optional aids. Unsupported
upstream contract modes are described in
[installation capabilities](references/installation-capabilities.md).

Work in the current authorized task. Role names describe review perspectives;
they do not require separate agents. Delegate only with independent task
authorization. External sending, journal submission, account actions and extra
knowledge-base writes require their own authorized scope. Preserve existing
approval; ask only for an unresolved decision that affects the work.

Use the user's language, evidence boundaries and document defaults. Match depth
to the requested artifact. Distinguish a draft, a simulated review, a verified
claim and a publication-ready result. Never claim independent or blind review
unless that procedure actually happened.
