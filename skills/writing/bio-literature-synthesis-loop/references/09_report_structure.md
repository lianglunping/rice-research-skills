# Report Structure

Write in Chinese by default. Keep gene names, software names, file names, variables, and identifiers in English.

## Required Report

1. **Scope**
   - Date range, source notes, Zotero collection, topic filter, and limitations.
2. **Literature State Snapshot**
   - New, updated, due, stale, missing audit, missing metadata.
3. **This-Cycle Synthesis**
   - Key learning changes, not just paper list.
4. **Topic Evidence Matrix**
   - Topics, papers, evidence types, strengths, weaknesses, next actions.
5. **Critical Lessons**
   - What other papers' weaknesses teach us about our own research design.
6. **Recall Questions**
   - Active-recall questions with answer keys and sources.
7. **Research Seed Backlog**
   - Ranked seeds with rationale and risks.
8. **Backtracking and External Update Queue**
   - Review-paper claims needing original-paper checks; stale claims needing search.
9. **Next-Cycle Reading Plan**
   - P0/P1/P2 reading priorities.
10. **Zotero/Obsidian Dry-Run Plan**
   - Proposed notes, tags, dashboards, review schedule.
11. **QA Self-Assessment**
   - Source coverage, missing inputs, unreliable notes, synthesis confidence.

End with: "如需把某个 topic evidence matrix 展开成专题综述、把某个 research seed 转成实验/生信验证计划，或生成下一轮阅读队列，请回复对应主题、seed 编号或时间范围。"
