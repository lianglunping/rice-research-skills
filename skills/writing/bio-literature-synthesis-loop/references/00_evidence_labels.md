# Evidence Labels

Use these labels consistently.

| Label | Meaning | Required anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[原文证据]` | Evidence carried over from original paper notes or figure cards | citekey, note path, figure/panel/table/section |
| `[原文结论]` | A conclusion recorded as the author's claim in a paper note | citekey and note section |
| `[原文重构]` | A synthesis from multiple notes or figures | source citekeys and note paths |
| `[外部检索补充]` | New external update found during synthesis | source, search date, query, DOI/title/URL |
| `[专家研判]` | Expert interpretation of cross-paper pattern, weakness, or research opportunity | basis and uncertainty |

Rules:

- Do not convert one paper's result into a field consensus.
- Do not convert a review's second-hand claim into primary evidence unless original papers were checked.
- Mark stale or conflicting claims instead of forcing agreement.
- Keep all new cross-paper conclusions source-linked.
