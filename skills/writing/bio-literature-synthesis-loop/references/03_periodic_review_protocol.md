# Periodic Review Protocol

Periodic synthesis should help retention and research planning, not just list papers.

## Frequencies

| Cycle | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| weekly | What was read, what matters, immediate recall, next reading queue |
| biweekly | Topic clustering, emerging patterns, missing audits, short-term research ideas |
| monthly | Consensus/controversy/gap update, method transfer, seed ranking |
| half-year | Field route map, stale claims, major shifts, proposal candidates |
| yearly | Research direction audit, accumulated lessons, long-term roadmap |
| project-specific | A targeted synthesis around gene, trait, method, crop, or experiment |

## Required Sections

1. New literature this cycle.
2. Completed deep reads.
3. Strong evidence gained.
4. Weak but interesting claims.
5. Corrected, contradicted, or stale claims.
6. Transferable lessons for the user's research.
7. Recall questions.
8. Research seed ranking changes.
9. Backtracking queue.
10. Next-cycle reading plan.

Avoid mechanical summaries. Prioritize changes in understanding.
