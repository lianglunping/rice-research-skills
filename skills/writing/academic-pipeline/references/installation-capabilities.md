The current SKILL.md is the supported local workflow. Its linked methodological
references are available locally. Legacy agent definitions and upstream protocol
documents are retained for provenance and specialist reference, but are not an
installed executable orchestrator and must not silently replace the local entry.

the sprint-contract schema, its check_sprint_contract.py validator, and frozen
writer/evaluator/reviewer templates. Local source copies were checked and also
lack these files. No substitute schema or acceptance result was fabricated.

If the user explicitly requests an upstream strict-contract, material-passport,
paper-blind panel or collaboration-score mode, report DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
before making that promise. Offer the supported evidence-based workflow with
its different guarantees, or recover a verified complete upstream package as a
separately scoped change. Do not label ordinary review as strict-contract PASS.
