# CNS Empirical Writing Overlay

## Activation

Use only with `style_profile = cns_empirical`, activated by an explicit request for CNS/high-impact-journal style or an explicit `Cell`, `Nature`, or `Science` target. Keep exactly one primary module. With `journal_target = unspecified`, use shared cross-journal principles. Do not activate this overlay automatically for thesis, Word, or routine polishing work.

## Non-Imitation Boundary

- Reorder evidence; do not mimic a named paper's wording, cadence, titles, or rhetoric.
- Preserve every identifier, number, unit, statistic, comparison, and uncertainty boundary.
- Compression removes repetition, not experimental conditions or material limitations.
- Never turn association, prediction, interaction, or NIL contrast into causation or regulation.

## Shared Section Contracts

| Section | Required evidence-led work | Do not do |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract | problem, direct result, bounded interpretation, stated relevance | add an untested mechanism or deployment claim |
| Introduction | problem, decisive gap, necessary prior context, study question | narrate a broad field history or imitate journal slogans |
| Results | sequence discovery, validation, mechanism, resource, and application functions only as supported | make the heading promise more than the data show |
| Discussion | explain what the evidence establishes, compare alternatives, state material limits and next validation | repeat results or convert a hypothesis into a conclusion |

Local conclusions belong where the evidence supports them. A caveat is required only when it is material to the inference; it is not a mandatory sentence in every paragraph.

## Conditional Journal Modifiers

| Target | Conditional modifier |
|---|---|
| Cell | emphasize a coherent causal chain only where perturbation and readout directly connect it |
| Nature | foreground the central advance and breadth while retaining the precise evidence boundary |
| Science | foreground the concise question, principal evidence, and generalizable implication without overextending application |
| unspecified | use the common contracts above; do not infer a journal-specific voice |

## Claim Calibration

| Evidence payload | Permitted wording | Not permitted without added evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Direct perturbation, rescue, or genetic evidence | “is required for”, “restored”, “genetically supports”, subject to the tested context | general mechanism beyond the tested system |
| Association, correlation, prediction, interaction screen, or NIL difference | “is associated with”, “correlates with”, “predicts”, “interacts with”, “is consistent with” | “causes”, “regulates”, “mediates”, or naming a regulator |
| Unvalidated breeding or application evidence | “is a candidate for validation”, “may inform selection”, “warrants field evaluation” | “ready for breeding”, “improves yield in production”, or deployment claims |

### Mandatory RED Boundaries

- A promoter variant plus differential ChIP signal cannot be written as “the variant causes altered binding” without allele-specific perturbation.
- A protein interaction plus association or NIL difference cannot establish a regulatory relationship or name a regulator without direct functional evidence.

## English and Chinese Guidance

English: lead with the tested comparison and use verbs matching the evidence band: `observed`, `associated`, `was consistent with`, `restored`, or `was required in the tested context`.

中文：先写比较对象和直接观察，再写有边界的解释；使用“相关”“提示”“与……一致”“在当前材料中支持”，不要把“互作”“差异”写成“调控”或“导致”。

## Final Checklist

- Is activation explicit and the primary module unique?
- Do all quantitative facts, identifiers, and comparison groups match the source?
- Does each causal or regulatory verb have direct perturbation, rescue, or genetic support?
- Are promoter-variant/ChIP and interaction/NIL claims kept below causal or regulatory strength unless directly tested?
- Are application statements bounded by validation and field evidence?
- Has copied, cargo-cult, or journal-mimicking wording been removed?
