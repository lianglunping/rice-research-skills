# Synthetic CNS Empirical Writing Example

## Evidence Payload

In a synthetic rice panel, haplotype H2 at `LOC_Os09g12340` was associated with a 14.2% higher seedling survival after 8 days at 8 °C than H1 (`n = 96` lines; `P = 0.004`). H2 carries a promoter variant at `chr09:1234567:G>A`. ChIP-qPCR measured lower occupancy of TF-X near the H2 promoter in sampled seedlings. TF-X and the encoded protein P9 interacted in yeast two-hybrid assays. Two near-isogenic lines differing across a 1.8-Mb interval showed the same survival direction. No allele-edited promoter, TF-X perturbation, rescue, or multi-location field test was performed.

## Improved Abstract Decision

“H2 at `LOC_Os09g12340` was associated with 14.2% higher seedling survival after 8 days at 8 °C in `n = 96` rice lines (`P = 0.004`) and carries `chr09:1234567:G>A`. The H2 promoter variant and lower TF-X occupancy nominate a testable regulatory hypothesis, while the current data do not establish an allele-specific effect or field utility.”

Why this works: it retains `14.2%`, `8 days`, `8 °C`, `n = 96`, `P = 0.004`, and `chr09:1234567:G>A`; it calls the promoter/ChIP observation a hypothesis rather than a causal mechanism.

## Improved Results Decision

Discovery: report the H1/H2 survival comparison. Validation: report the direction in the two near-isogenic lines and name the 1.8-Mb interval. Mechanism-oriented observation: report lower TF-X occupancy and the yeast two-hybrid interaction separately. Do not write that `chr09:1234567:G>A` causes altered TF-X binding, or that TF-X regulates P9: neither claim has allele-specific perturbation or direct functional support.

## Improved Discussion Decision

中文示例：“H2 与低温存活率升高相关，且其启动子区域观察到较低的 TF-X 占据，提示该位点可能参与低温响应的调控网络。由于近等基因系仍包含 1.8-Mb 区间、未进行等位基因编辑或 TF-X 功能扰动，目前不能判定该启动子变异导致结合差异，也不能将 TF-X 定义为 P9 的调控因子。该单倍型可作为后续精细定位、等位基因验证和多点田间评价的候选材料。”
