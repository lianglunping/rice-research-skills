# Example: De-AI Rewrite

## Before

该结果具有重要意义，进一步揭示了重离子诱变在水稻基因组中的潜在作用机制，并为后续育种应用提供了坚实基础。

## After

## What Changed

- removed empty significance language
- replaced vague mechanism claim with an evidence-bounded statement
- kept the forward-looking sentence, but downgraded it to a justified scope
