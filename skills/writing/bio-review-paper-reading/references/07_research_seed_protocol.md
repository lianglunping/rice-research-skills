# Research Seed Protocol

Generate actionable, risk-aware ideas, not vague slogans.

## Seed Schema

```yaml
seed_id:
title:
source: review_gap|controversy|method_transfer|external_update|expert_judgment
core_question:
hypothesis:
why_now:
required_data:
minimum_analysis:
minimum_validation_experiment:
rice_relevance:
mutagenesis_relevance:
bioinformatics_workload:
wet_lab_workload:
novelty_risk:
feasibility:
publication_potential:
failure_modes:
next_action:
```

## Ranking

Use:

- novelty,
- feasibility,
- data availability,
- wet-lab validation cost,
- dry-lab workload,
- rice or crop relevance,
- mutagenesis relevance,
- risk of being incremental,
- time to minimum result.

Prefer ideas that can be tested by a small first step.
