# Evidence Labels

Use these labels consistently.

| Label | Meaning | Required anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[原文证据]` | Direct text, figure, table, citation cluster, or statement from the review PDF | Section, page, figure/table, or short excerpt |
| `[原文结论]` | A view explicitly stated by review authors | Section or quoted claim location |
| `[原文重构]` | A framework reconstructed from multiple review sections | Supporting original anchors |
| `[外部检索补充]` | Post-publication progress, new papers, updated tools, author-team follow-up, citation context | Search source, date, title/DOI/URL |
| `[专家研判]` | Expert judgment on evidence strength, bias, field trajectory, or research opportunity | Basis and uncertainty |

Rules:

- A review citation is not automatically primary evidence.
- Mark `需回源` when a key claim depends on cited original papers that have not been checked.
- Use `原文未系统展开` when the review only touches a topic lightly.
- Use `以下为基于原文信息的结构化重构` when the framework is reconstructed rather than explicit.
- Use `【图表解析受限】` if conceptual figures or tables are unreadable.
