# Review Type Triage

Classify before interpretation.

| Type | Signals | Reading stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `narrative_review` | broad synthesis, no explicit search protocol | Useful framing but possible selection bias |
| `systematic_review` | search strategy, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screening flow | Use systematic-review lens; do not assume quality without checking methods |
| `scoping_review` | maps evidence scope and concepts | Good for field boundary and gap map |
| `perspective` | viewpoint, opinion, future directions | Treat as author argument, not field consensus |
| `opinion` | explicitly argumentative | Identify bias and assumptions |
| `roadmap` | future challenges and priorities | Extract research route and feasibility assumptions |
| `primer` | introductory synthesis | Good for glossary and concept hierarchy |

If type is uncertain, report the uncertainty and continue with cautious narrative-review assumptions.

Check:

- Does the paper have Methods for literature search?
- Are inclusion/exclusion criteria stated?
- Are cited studies systematically selected or illustrative?
- Is the author promoting a specific model, technology, or research program?
