# Review Figures and Tables

Review figures are usually frameworks, models, routes, or comparisons. They are not direct experiments unless explicitly based on original data.

Some review figures are hybrids: one panel may show an illustrative experimental image or reporter output, while another panel presents a conceptual model. Treat these as mixed figures rather than forcing a single label.

## Visual Language

Decode:

- arrows, dashed arrows, inhibition bars,
- cell compartments,
- pathway modules,
- data-flow direction,
- technology pipeline stages,
- color groups,
- hierarchy levels,
- input and output nodes,
- uncertainty markers.

Always distinguish established mechanism from author's integrative model.

## Hybrid Figure Rule

When a review figure mixes evidence-like panels and conceptual diagrams:

- label the figure `hybrid_conceptual`,
- describe which panels are illustrative examples versus which panels are conceptual synthesis,
- avoid treating the figure as primary experimental proof unless the review explicitly presents original data.
