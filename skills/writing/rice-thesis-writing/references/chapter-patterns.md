# Chapter Patterns

Use this file when the user asks for thesis chapter integration or polishing.

## Literature review chapter

The literature review should usually separate these functions:

1. research development and theoretical framing,
2. current status in the target field or crop,
3. mechanism or method comparisons,
4. major debates or unresolved questions,
5. knowledge gaps,
6. the study's positioning, objectives, or significance.

### Do

- Rebuild scattered drafts into one stable heading tree.
- Compress overlapping material across source drafts.
- Turn workbench labels into normal thesis prose.
- Keep “knowledge gap” distinct from “research objective”.

### Do not

- Leave prompt-like headings such as `执行摘要`, `整合摘要`, `可直接写入论文`, `输出部分`, or `建议写入论文背景`.
- Mix “current consensus”, “mechanistic explanation”, and “the present study” into the same paragraph without a clear transition.
- Introduce a new section that the source material does not support.

## Introduction or thesis-background sections

- Move from broad field importance to the narrower problem.
- Use the literature to justify the scientific question, not to oversell the study.
- End with the study's objective, problem statement, or significance only after the gap has been established.

## Discussion sections

- Separate evidence-supported findings from interpretation.
- Compare with literature carefully and mark inference as inference.
- Emphasize limitations, conditions, and what remains unresolved.

## Transitional writing rules

- Use restrained transitions such as:
  - `综上所述，`
  - `已有研究表明，`
  - `然而，`
  - `在此基础上，`
  - `因此，`
- Avoid slogans, publicity language, and template-heavy filler.
- If multiple source drafts say the same thing, keep the version with the strongest evidence chain and best thesis fit.
