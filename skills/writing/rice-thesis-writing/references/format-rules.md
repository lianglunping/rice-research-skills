# Thesis Format Rules

Use these rules when the user asks for thesis-ready Chinese正文 or Word-format normalization.

## Core principle

- Chinese text uses the required Chinese font.
- English words, abbreviations, Latin names, Arabic numerals, DOI, URL, and English punctuation use Times New Roman.
- English font follows the same size, boldness, alignment, and spacing as the surrounding Chinese content.

## Heading hierarchy

- Thesis title:
  - Chinese font: 黑体
  - English font: Times New Roman
  - Size: 一号
  - Align: centered
  - Line spacing: 1.5 lines
  - Not part of the numbered heading tree
- Level 1 heading:
  - Chinese font: 黑体
  - English font: Times New Roman
  - Size: 小二
  - Align: centered
  - Outline level: 1
  - Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
  - Before: 0.5 line
  - After: 1.5 lines
- Level 2 heading:
  - Chinese font: 黑体
  - English font: Times New Roman
  - Size: 小三
  - Align: justified
  - Outline level: 2
  - Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
  - Before: 12 pt
  - After: 12 pt
- Level 3 heading:
  - Chinese font: 黑体
  - English font: Times New Roman
  - Size: 小四
  - Bold
  - Align: justified
  - Outline level: 3
  - Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
  - Before: 6 pt
  - After: 6 pt

## Body text

- Chinese font: 宋体
- English font: Times New Roman
- Size: 小四
- Align: justified
- Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
- First-line indent: 2 characters
- Before: 0
- After: 0

## Numbering

- Default hierarchy:
  - Level 1: `1`, `2`, `3`
  - Level 2: `1.1`, `1.2`, `1.3`
  - Level 3: `1.1.1`, `1.1.2`, `1.1.3`
- Abstract, keywords, references, acknowledgements, and format notes may remain unnumbered.
- Do not introduce more than three heading levels unless the user explicitly requires it.

## Figures and tables

- Figure captions below figures.
- Table captions above tables.
- Font: 宋体 for Chinese, Times New Roman for English.
- Size: 小四
- Align: centered
- Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
- Numbering by chapter:
  - `图 1-1`, `图 1-2`
  - `表 2-1`, `表 2-2`
- Appendix numbering:
  - `图 A-1`
  - `表 A-1`

## Reference list formatting

- Chinese font: 宋体
- English font: Times New Roman
- Size: 小四
- Align: justified
- Line spacing: fixed 20 pt
- Hanging indent: 2 characters

## Mixed Chinese-English cleanup

- Use Chinese full-width punctuation in Chinese prose.
- Fix stray spaces between Chinese and punctuation.
- Keep technical English terms, database IDs, DOI, URL, and Latin names in their original language.
- Remove markdown residue such as `#`, `##`, `-`, `*`, `>`, and triple backticks.
- Remove meaningless separators, duplicated blank lines, and chat or webpage copy artifacts.
