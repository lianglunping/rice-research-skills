# Discussion Close Reading

Discussion is often where the paper's real intellectual value and overreach both appear. Read it separately after Results/Figures.

## Discussion Audit Table

Use this structure:

| Discussion claim | Author interpretation | Directly proven by Results? | Inference chain | Possible overreach | Needed follow-up | External check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Questions To Ask

- What does the author most want the reader to remember?
- Which statements are directly supported by figures?
- Which statements are interpretation, model building, or future hypothesis?
- Does the author acknowledge limitations, or hide them in cautious phrasing?
- Are single genotype, tissue, environment, or time-point findings generalized too widely?
- Are correlation, expression, or association results presented as causal mechanism?
- Are breeding implications supported by field, multi-environment, or population evidence?
- Are negative results, failed candidates, weak panels, or alternative models absent?

## Post-Publication Link

When external search is allowed, use it to check whether major Discussion claims were later:

- independently supported,
- refined,
- contradicted,
- replaced by better technology,
- or still unresolved.

Label those findings `[外部检索补充]`; do not rewrite original-paper conclusions.

## Recency Rule

If the paper is very recent and no meaningful follow-up literature exists yet, state that explicitly:

- `No substantive post-publication follow-up signal was identified yet because the paper is too recent.`

Do not force a "latest progress" narrative from journal pages, alert pages, or weak citation traces alone.

## Mini Example

| Discussion claim | Author interpretation | Directly proven by Results? | Inference chain | Possible overreach | Needed follow-up | External check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GeneX is a promising breeding target for drought tolerance | GeneX mutation improves drought-related phenotype | Partly: phenotype shown in one background and one condition | mutant phenotype -> GeneX function -> breeding utility | Field, elite-background, and yield-penalty evidence may be missing | multi-environment validation and near-isogenic line test | Search later GeneX field/breeding studies if external search is allowed |
