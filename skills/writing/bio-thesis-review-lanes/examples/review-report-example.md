# Example Review Report

## External Lane

1. [major] Section 2.4 positions the study as mechanism-resolving, but the presented evidence is still descriptive.
Why it matters: an external reviewer may treat the chapter as overclaiming and discount later interpretations.
Minimum fix: replace mechanism-final wording with a bounded statement that the current data support a chromatin-associated pattern rather than a demonstrated mechanism.

## Advisor Lane

1. [major] The chapter spends too much space on general mutagenesis background before introducing the specific rice heavy-ion problem.
Why it matters: the main thesis line appears late and the chapter reads broader than necessary.
Minimum fix: compress the general background and move the rice-specific question two subsections earlier.

## Committee Lane

1. [critical] The chapter does not clearly state what counts as the dissertation's original contribution versus what remains a resource or hypothesis layer.
Why it matters: this is a likely pre-defense attack point because the committee will ask what exactly is doctoral-level and already established.
Minimum fix: add a short contribution-boundary subsection that separates catalog construction, annotation/prioritization, and mechanism-oriented exploratory analyses.
