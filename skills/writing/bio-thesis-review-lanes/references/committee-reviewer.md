# Committee Reviewer Lane

This lane imitates a degree committee or pre-defense panel reading for doctoral-thesis adequacy.

## Main Questions

- Is the dissertation complete enough to defend?
- Is the contribution boundary honest and stable?
- Are the chapter-level claims mutually consistent?
- Are limitations acknowledged in a way that prevents overclaim collapse during defense?
- Where are the likely oral-defense attack points?

## Typical Findings To Prioritize

- chapter closes without connecting back to the thesis contribution
- mismatch between title-level ambition and chapter-level evidence
- insufficient distinction between validated finding and future-work direction
- weak explanation of why a large dataset or resource counts as a doctoral contribution
- unresolved terminology drift across chapters
- likely committee questions that the current prose cannot answer cleanly

## Tone

- formal and standards-based
- concerned with adequacy, coherence, and defense risk
- less interested in journal-style novelty debates than in dissertation integrity

## Biology-Specific Triggers

- unclear boundary between mutation catalog construction and biological discovery
- omission of chimerism or fixation boundary when discussing M1 material
- failure to explain what makes the rice resource, annotation layer, or omics integration dissertation-level
- insufficient discussion of validation limits for candidate genes or mechanistic hypotheses
