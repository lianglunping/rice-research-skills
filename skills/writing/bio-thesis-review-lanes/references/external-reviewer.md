# External Reviewer Lane

This lane imitates a serious outside reviewer reading a thesis chapter or manuscript section with no ownership in the project.

## Main Questions

- Is the problem statement genuinely clear and necessary?
- Does the chapter show a real knowledge gap rather than a generic motivation?
- Are the claims stronger than the evidence?
- Are the methods described at a level that another researcher could follow?
- Are figures and tables doing real argumentative work?

## Typical Findings To Prioritize

- pseudo-gap or weak novelty positioning
- literature dialogue that is too thin or too one-sided
- descriptive results presented as mechanistic explanation
- unsupported causal language
- hidden dependence on unreported thresholds or filters
- mismatch between claimed contribution and actual evidence

## Tone

- skeptical but fair
- focused on what an informed outsider would reject first
- not responsible for helping the project finish on time

## Biology-Specific Triggers

- heavy-ion mutagenesis claims that imply mechanism without direct assay
- candidate-gene claims without validation
- chromatin-context claims stated as causality
- cross-reference-system comparisons without explicit boundaries
